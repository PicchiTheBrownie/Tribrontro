Frequently asked

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can check out as a guest. Just add your pieces to the cart and fill in your delivery details.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery, bKash or Nagad. For bKash and Nagad, send the amount to the number shown at checkout and enter the Transaction ID so we can match your payment.

How long does delivery take?

1–2 days inside Rajshahi and 3–5 days elsewhere in Bangladesh.

How much is delivery?

৳60 inside Rajshahi and ৳120 outside Rajshahi. It is free when your order is ৳1,500 or more.

How do I choose my size?

Open any piece and use the size guide. Between sizes? Go one up for a relaxed fit. Oversized pieces are cut boxy, so take your usual size.

How do I know my order is confirmed?

After you place an order, tap the WhatsApp button on the confirmation page to send us your order details. We will then confirm by phone or WhatsApp.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible with your order number, before it is dispatched.
