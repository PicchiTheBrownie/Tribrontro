Privacy policy

What we collect. Your name, phone number, delivery address, order details and, if you give it, your email. We only ask for what we need to deliver your order.

How we use it. To process and deliver your order, to contact you about it, and to send our newsletter only if you subscribe.

Where it is kept. Your cart, saved pieces and saved details are stored in your own browser on your device. Your order reaches us when you send it to us on WhatsApp.

Sharing. We share your name, phone and address with our delivery partners only so they can deliver your order. We never sell your data.

Your choices. You can clear your saved details at any time from your account page, or ask us to delete anything we hold about you.
